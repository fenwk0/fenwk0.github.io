Why Ada?

Ada was designed in the late 1970s for large, long-lived systems. Its strong typing, built-in tasking and protected objects, and run-time checks catch many classes of errors early. The Ada Information Clearinghouse and Alire are good places to learn more about the language and its ecosystem.
